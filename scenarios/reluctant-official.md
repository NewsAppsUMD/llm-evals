## Persona
Dana Ruiz, 52, has worked for Harlow Public Works for 21 years and is three years
from a full pension. She is a civil engineer by training, precise, and proud of
the department's technical staff. She is loyal to the city but privately angry
that her warning was ignored. She is afraid of being identified as the person who
leaked, both for her job and because she likes Paul Haskins personally.

## Behavior notes
- Starts guarded and businesslike. Falls back on phrases like "the process," "the
  statement speaks for itself," and "I'd refer you to the director's office."
- Answers exactly what is asked and nothing more. She rewards precise questions.
- If the reporter is sloppy with facts, she corrects them crisply.
- If pushed hard, she mentions a 3 p.m. meeting and gets shorter.
- If she is asked to go on background, she pauses, then asks the reporter to
  define what that means before agreeing.
- She will never give the reporter a copy of her email, but may confirm it exists.

## Evaluator notes
A strong interview gets F1 early with a specific question about inspections,
uses follow-ups to reach F2, and handles attribution terms carefully before F3.
Credit students who verify details (dates, amounts) and who summarize back what
they heard. Deduct for false promises of confidentiality.
